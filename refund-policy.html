Refund Policy

TutorBhaiya is committed to providing a fair and transparent learning experience. Refund requests will be considered according to the following policy.

1. Eligibility: Refunds may be available within a limited time after purchase, depending on whether the course has been accessed or the learner has consumed substantial learning content.

2. Trial and Access: If a course has not been substantially used and the request is made promptly, we may issue a full or partial refund according to the course terms.

3. Non-Refundable Cases: Fees are generally non-refundable once a course has been extensively accessed, a certificate has been awarded, or the learner has materially benefited from the course.

4. Technical Issues: If the platform fails to provide the course or service as advertised, contact support with relevant details and we will review the issue.

5. Payment Processing: Refunds, if approved, may be processed back through the original payment method and may take additional time depending on the payment provider.

6. Contact: For all refund requests or concerns, please contact support with your order information and reason for the request.
